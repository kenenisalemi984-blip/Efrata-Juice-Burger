OUR STORY & CRAFT

Where Fresh Harvest Meets Culinary Passion.

Founded with a simple promise—to serve wholesome, vibrant, and deeply satisfying food—Efrata Juice & Burger has become a beloved gathering place for families, friends, and food lovers.

Every morning begins with hand-selecting sun-ripened avocados, mangoes, papayas, strawberries, and crisp garden greens alongside premium cuts of beef and chicken. Nothing is pre-bottled or rushed; every burger is seared to order and every juice is pressed fresh when you ask for it.

Farm-Fresh Ingredients

We partner with local growers for peak-season fruits, crisp lettuce, ripe tomatoes, and aromatic herbs delivered daily.

Uncompromising Quality

100% pure fruit juices with zero artificial flavors, paired with freshly seasoned patties and house-made sauces.

Guest Satisfaction

Warm Ethiopian hospitality is at the heart of every table we serve, ensuring fast, attentive, and friendly care.

Our Mission & Values

To nourish our community with honest, vibrant, and memorable meals that bring people together every single day.
